What to notice
  • From a given \(k_0\), only one \(c_0\) leads to \((k^*,c^*)\). Every other choice diverges: too high and \(k\) runs out, too low and \(c\) collapses toward zero.
  • \(k^*\lt k_{gold}\): the planner discounts, so it does not push capital all the way to the golden rule.
Fig. 4Why the transversality condition picks the saddle path

What to notice
  • Too low: capital piles up while \(c\to0\), so \(\mu k\) grows and the TVC fails: capital is left unused forever.
  • Too high: \(k\) hits zero in finite time, and the path is infeasible.
  • Saddle: \(\mu k\) falls to zero, so the TVC holds.
