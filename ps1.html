Exercise 2.11Solow with land in fixed supply

\[ F=L^{\beta}K^{\alpha}Z^{1-\alpha-\beta}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dot k = s\,(Z/L)^{1-\alpha-\beta}k^{\alpha}-(n+\delta)\,k. \]

(b) Population growth

With \(L=e^{nt}\), land per worker shrinks and \(g(k,t)=sBe^{-\gamma t}k^{\alpha-1}-(n+\delta)\), with \(\gamma=(1-\alpha-\beta)n\). Its zero, the temporary steady state \(k^*(t)\), now moves.

(c) Malthusian population

Let population growth respond to income: \(n=n(y)\) with \(n'>0\) and \(n(\bar y)=0\). The figure uses \(n(y)=\bar n\,(1-\bar y/y)\).

Exercise 2.12Only capitalists save

Workers consume everything; capitalists save a share \(s_K\) of capital income: \[\dot k = s_K\,f'(k)\,k-(n+\delta)k,\qquad f(k)=k^{\alpha}.\] The golden rule \(k_{gold}\) solves \(f'(k)=n+\delta\).
